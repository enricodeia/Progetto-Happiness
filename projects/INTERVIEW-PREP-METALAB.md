### 3. "How do you translate complex product work into compelling marketing stories?"

Questa e LA domanda chiave per il ruolo. Rispondi con un framework, non un aneddoto vago.

> "I think about it in three layers.
>
> First, **find the human story inside the product story**. Every product exists because someone had a problem. The case study isn't about features — it's about the transformation. What was broken before? What changed?
>
> Second, **show the thinking, not just the output**. The most compelling stories show decisions — why this direction and not that one. Process artifacts, explorations, the moments where the work got messy before it got good.
>
> Third, **let the craft speak**. Full-bleed imagery, detail crops of typography and spacing, motion captures of interactions. The quality of how you present the work IS the proof that the work is good.
>
> And because I can code, I can actually build interactive case studies — not just static pages. Scroll-driven animations, embedded prototypes, responsive layouts that feel like products themselves. The case study becomes an experience, not just a document."

**Perche funziona:** Dai un framework chiaro (3 layer), lo chiudi con il tuo differentiatore (coding), e mostri che pensi come Metalab pensa.

### 4. "How do you use AI in your design workflow?"

Metalab e AI-forward. Sara Vienna parla di "designing a more human relationship with AI." Questa domanda e quasi garantita.

> "AI is embedded in my workflow at almost every stage, but I use it intentionally — not as a replacement for thinking, but as an accelerator for iteration.
>
> For **visual exploration**, I use Midjourney and DALL-E to generate mood boards and direction options in minutes instead of hours. It's not about using AI output directly — it's about expanding the creative territory I can explore before committing to a direction.
>
> For **strategy and copywriting**, I use Claude as a thinking partner — briefing it with context, having it challenge my positioning, generating headline options. It's like having a strategist on call.
>
> For **implementation**, I use AI-assisted coding — Claude Code for building landing pages and interactive case studies in React. I can go from a Figma design to a live, responsive page in a fraction of the time.
>
> For **design iteration**, Figma AI helps me quickly generate layout variations and component options.
>
> The key is that AI amplifies craft — it doesn't replace it. The taste, the editorial eye, the narrative structure — that's still human. AI just lets me explore more and execute faster."

**Perche funziona:** Sei specifico (tool names, use cases), non generico. Mostri che usi AI per accelerare, non per pigrizia. E chiudi con la filosofia giusta — craft is still human.

### 5. "Tell us about a time you pushed back on creative direction."

Metalab vuole designer con opinioni forti ma ego controllato.

> "On a recent branding project, the client initially wanted a very trendy, maximalist direction — heavy gradients, 3D elements, lots of visual noise. I understood the instinct — they wanted to look 'current.'
>
> But after studying their audience and competitors, I realized that approach would make them look exactly like everyone else in their space. So I pushed back — not by saying 'I don't like it,' but by showing them three directions: what they asked for, a middle ground, and a stripped-back approach with strong typography and intentional whitespace.
>
> I presented each with strategic reasoning — who it speaks to, how it ages, how it scales across touchpoints. The client chose the cleaner direction. Not because I convinced them, but because the rationale was clear.
>
> The lesson: pushing back isn't about taste — it's about evidence. Show, don't argue."

**Adatta questo:** Usa un tuo progetto reale. Stessa struttura: situazione > il tuo pushback > come lo hai presentato > risultato.

### 6. "How do you balance brand consistency with creative experimentation?"

Questa tocca il "seasonal brand" concept di Metalab.

> "I think the best brand systems are designed with flex built in. You define what's fixed — the core identity elements, the typography, the fundamental rules — and you define where there's room to play.
>
> Metalab's own seasonal brand approach is a great example of this. The wordmark and custom typeface stay constant. But the 3D star, the textures, the secondary elements evolve. That's a system that's alive without being inconsistent.
>
> In my work, I build brand guidelines that explicitly define the 'fixed' and 'flex' zones. The logo, type scale, and color system are sacred. But photography direction, illustration style, and motion language can evolve with campaigns or seasons.
>
> The danger is when everything is fixed — the brand becomes a cage. Or when nothing is fixed — the brand becomes noise. The skill is drawing that line in the right place."

**Perche funziona:** Referenzi il loro seasonal brand concept — dimostri che hai studiato. E dai una risposta che mostra systems thinking maturo.

### 7. "What's your process for a case study from start to finish?"

Il core deliverable del ruolo. Sii specifico.

> "I follow a five-phase process:
>
> **1. Story mining.** I interview the project team — the designer, the strategist, the client partner. I ask: what was the real challenge? What was the moment everything clicked? What decision are you most proud of? I'm looking for the emotional core of the story.
>
> **2. Narrative structure.** I write the story before I design anything. Hook, challenge, insight, approach, craft, outcome. If the story doesn't work in text, it won't work visually.
>
> **3. Visual direction.** Hero imagery, detail crops, motion captures. I decide what needs to be photographed, screen-recorded, or animated. I think about pacing — where does the viewer need a full-bleed cinematic moment, and where do they need a quiet detail?
>
> **4. Design & build.** In Figma first, then I build it — usually in React with scroll-driven animations and responsive layouts. Because I code, the case study itself becomes a product-quality experience.
>
> **5. Polish & iteration.** Kerning, transitions, load performance, mobile experience. The case study has to be as good as the work it showcases."

**Perche funziona:** Processo chiaro in 5 fasi, include story mining (narrative thinking), e il fatto che tu costruisca in React e un differentiatore enorme.

## Red Flags da Evitare

| Non fare | Fai invece |
|---|---|
| Parlare solo di estetica ("I love beautiful design") | Parla di narrativa, strategia, impatto |
| Essere vago sul processo | Dai framework specifici e step concreti |
| Sottovalutare il coding | E il tuo superpotere — menzionalo piu volte |
| Parlare troppo di te | Collega ogni risposta a come aiuteresti Metalab |
| Essere troppo umile | Sii confident — "I own the direction" non "I try my best" |
| Ignorare l'AI | Metalab e AI-forward, mostra che lo sei anche tu |
| Non conoscere il loro lavoro recente | Windsurf, Suno, seasonal brand — devi saperli |

## Power Moves

1. **Apri il portfolio durante il call.** Non aspettare che te lo chiedano — quando parli di un progetto, condividi schermo e mostralo.

2. **Usa il linguaggio di Metalab.** "Narrative-driven." "Craft." "Story-first." "Product-informed." Queste parole sono nel job posting — usale naturalmente.

3. **Menziona il coding almeno 3 volte.** In contesti diversi: case study interattivi, prototipi, landing page. Non come skill tecnica — come superpotere creativo.

4. **Cita Sara Vienna.** Una volta, naturalmente. Mostra che conosci la leadership.

5. **Chiudi forte.** L'ultima cosa che dici conta piu di tutto il resto. Prepara una closing line:
   > "I genuinely believe this is the role I've been building toward. The combination of narrative design, product thinking, and technical craft — that's exactly where I want to be. I'm excited about the possibility of shaping how Metalab shows up in the world."
