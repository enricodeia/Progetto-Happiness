# Metalab — Sliced Monolith

Creative direction for a visual campaign exploring the Metalab brand as a
sculptural, monolithic form. Built for the Senior Brand Designer application.

## The logo

A sharp four-pointed geometric star. Four tall triangular spikes pointing in
the cardinal directions, with concave angular cuts between them. Strong,
symmetric, tense. Reads both as a compass rose and as a solid sculptural mass.

`assets/logo.svg` · white on transparent.

## The concept: SLICED MONOLITH

The Metalab logo rendered as a **monolith of raw matter**, horizontally
**sliced into thin parallel layers**, with **ultraviolet plasma** emerging
from between the sections like an inner life force.

Three tensions:

1. **MASS** — stone, concrete, granite, obsidian → boldness, heavy product
2. **SLICE** — horizontal cross-sectioning → precision, systematic thinking
3. **PLASMA** — UV light between layers → intelligence, the soul inside form

The story: Metalab takes raw matter (the brief) and shapes it with system
(the slicing), revealing intelligence (plasma) inside.

## Visual cues (from references)

- Thin wireframe boundary box around each sculpture (blueprint × object)
- Horizontal CT-scan style slicing
- Raw material surfaces (hewn, weathered, cast)
- Dramatic rim / ultraviolet lighting
- Cinematic depth of field, gaussian falloff on edges
- Black, white, or landscape backgrounds — never busy

## Visual grammar across all shots

- Logo mass always front and center, slight three-quarter angle
- Horizontal slices evenly spaced (8-14 slices)
- Thin white/cyan wireframe boundary box
- UV plasma glow between slices, volumetric, soft bloom
- Shallow DoF, subtle atmospheric haze
- No text, no additional elements

## Fallback plan for logo fidelity

Pure text prompts won't reproduce the exact Metalab star perfectly. Strategy:
1. Generate with rich shape description
2. Pick best shots
3. Composite real SVG logo in Figma for client-presentable versions
