Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. What We Collect

Pairo collects the following data when you use the platform:

  • Account data: email address, name, password (hashed)
  • Profile data: role, company, location, skills, bio, photos, date of birth, prompts
  • Activity data: swipes, matches, messages, saved profiles
  • Preferences: distance, role types, industries, notification settings
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID but never your card details.

2. How We Use It

  • Display your profile to other users in the discover feed
  • Match you with compatible professionals based on your preferences
  • Enable messaging between matched users
  • Process Pro subscription payments via Stripe
  • Improve the matching algorithm

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security enabled. Photos are stored in Supabase Storage. All connections use HTTPS.

4. Who Sees Your Data

  • Other users see your public profile (name, role, photos, skills, bio, prompts). They cannot see your email, Stripe data, or preferences.
  • Matched users can also see your messages.
  • No one sees your swipe history except you.

5. Third Parties

  • Supabase: authentication, database, file storage
  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Google: OAuth sign-in (if you choose to use it)
  • Vercel: hosting

We do not sell your data to anyone.

6. Your Rights

  • View and edit your profile data at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data on request
  • Control profile visibility in Settings

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (session tokens). No tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

8. Data Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all data is removed within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated in-app.

10. Contact

For privacy questions: hello@pairo.app
